🌱 MarketLink kya business hai?

MarketLink ek online marketplace/platform hai jo local farmers aur customers ko connect karta hai.

Farmer → MarketLink → Customer

Farmer apne available products (jaise vegetables/fruits) MarketLink par list karta hai. Customer website par products dekhta hai, order/pre-order karta hai aur market se pickup karta hai.

👨‍🌾 Farmer side

Farmer:

Apna account banata hai
Apne products add karta hai
Price aur stock update karta hai
Kis market mein product available hai, woh assign hota hai
Customer ka order accept/decline karta hai
Order ko Ready aur phir Completed karta hai
Stock khatam hone par product Sold Out hota hai
Dobara stock aane par favorited customers ko notification mil sakti hai
🛒 Customer side

Customer:

Products search/filter karta hai
Price, category aur market ke according products dekhta hai
Product ko favorite karta hai
Cart mein add karta hai
Pickup date/time select karke order karta hai
Order status track karta hai:
Placed → Accepted → Ready → Completed
Order history aur reviews dekh sakta hai
Product unavailable ho to restock notification receive kar sakta hai
👨‍💼 Admin ka business role

Admin poore platform ko manage karta hai:

Farmers
Customers
Markets
Products
Orders
Reviews
Reports
Farmer activation/suspension
💰 Paisa/business model

Tumhare current project ke according online payment gateway nahi hai aur delivery bhi nahi hai.

Isliye business model ka basic flow:

Customer product order karta hai → farmer product ready karta hai → customer market se pickup karta hai → payment in-person hoti hai.

Yani MarketLink khud primarily connecting/management platform hai; current design mein website par online payment collect nahi ho rahi.

Ek line mein presentation ke liye:

“MarketLink is a digital marketplace that connects local farmers with customers, allowing farmers to manage their products and orders while customers can discover, pre-order, and pick up products from local markets.”
